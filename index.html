See every fine. Pay it before it doubles. — Traffic fine checker & payer for Pakistan

SAMPLE DATA — every fine, plate, challan, figure and person shown here is invented for this UX concept demo. No real fine, plate, challan, or person appears. Challan is an unofficial concept, not a traffic-police app: always check the challan and the plate before you pay anyone.

CHALLAN BOARD

See every fine. Pay it before it doubles.

In Pakistan, e-challans are issued by traffic cameras — but the notice arrives late or never. Ahmed Raza, 34, from Lahore, found out about a Rs 2,000 overspeeding challan only when his licence renewal was blocked. Type a plate and see every fine before the due date.

PAY A FINE

Rs 2,000 fine. Pay it in 2 minutes.

Pick an unpaid challan and pay it online with JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank — no agent, no cash, no Rs 1,500 "clearing" fee. The official cost is just the fine. Payment mints a receipt you can show at the traffic office.

DISPUTE

Wrong plate? Dispute in one tap.

Every challan carries a camera photo. If the photo plate does not match your plate, the camera caught the wrong car — dispute it instead of paying it. Sample case: challan CH-2026-902 shows a white Corolla, photo plate LHR-9902 — not Ahmed Raza's LHR-4812.

REMINDERS

Never pay late again.

Challan reminds you 3 days before each due date — CH-2026-310 is due 17 Nov 2026, so its reminder is 14 Nov 2026; CH-2026-4812 is due 26 Nov 2026, so its reminder is 23 Nov 2026. Fog season means more camera challans. Your reminders are saved on this device.

    AGENT SPOTTER

    Spot the agent overcharge.

    An agent outside the traffic office asked Rs 1,500 to "clear" a Rs 2,000 challan. The official cost is just the Rs 2,000 fine — the fee is pure overcharge. Three things an agent does that should stop you paying him — and one safe habit. Call each one a red flag or safe, then check your score.
